## Design Context

### Users
Product and UX designers preparing for interviews. They use a TRMNL e-ink display as a quick daily prompt, then work through an exercise at a scope that fits their experience level.

### Brand Personality
Focused, editorial, and disciplined. The deck should feel like a well-composed studio brief: confident and practical, with enough character to feel motivating but no visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
A type-led, high-contrast monochrome system designed for glanceability on a 1-bit display. Use crisp hierarchy, compact line icons, restrained dividers, and generous whitespace. Avoid decorative color, soft low-contrast text, rounded-card UI, and unnecessary ornament.

### Design Principles
- Make the daily challenge and its practice level understandable at a glance.
- Keep instructional headings dark and distinct from supporting copy.
- Preserve legibility and hierarchy on 1-bit e-ink hardware.
- Use typography, spacing, and rules—not color—to organize information.
- Keep the visual language consistent across all four TRMNL layouts.
